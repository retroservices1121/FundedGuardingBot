Privacy Policy

Your account. On the go. This policy explains the information Funded Guardian uses to provide its mobile app.

Last updated: October 7, 2026 · Applies to the Funded Guardian mobile app and these support pages.

Information we use

  • Sign-in information. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a provider account identifier and, when provided, your email address. We use these to create and authenticate your Guardian profile. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Your connected account. If you connect MyFundedPerps, we store an encrypted copy of the API key, its last four characters, the selected account identifier, and your live or sandbox environment. Guardian uses the key on its server to request account information and perform the trading actions you initiate.
  • Account and trading information. We retrieve balances, account rules, positions, orders, prices, fees and trading history to display your account and calculate trade estimates. We store trade and position-edit review records, confirmation status and results to process your requests and prevent duplicate submissions.
  • Preferences. We store your guardrail settings and quick-trade preferences. Notification choices are saved on your device and with your registered device on our server.
  • Device and session information. We use authentication sessions and, if you enable notifications, a device push token and delivery records. Your device stores a session token and notification settings to keep you signed in and remember your choices.
  • Support messages. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include so we can investigate and respond.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, connect your account, display account activity, calculate and review trades, send notifications you choose, and resolve support requests. Account rules and trade acceptance are determined by MyFundedPerps.

The public privacy and support pages do not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting service processes network requests to deliver the pages.

Services involved

  • Apple and Google authenticate your sign-in. Apple also distributes the iOS app and delivers notifications through its push service.
  • MyFundedPerps receives authenticated account requests and the trading instructions you initiate. Market data services provide chart and price information.
  • Expo provides app updates and push notification delivery. Push delivery uses your device token and notification content.
  • Railway hosts the Guardian backend and its database.
  • Share destinations receive a trade card only when you choose to share it. The card contains trade details and P&L; it excludes your API key and account balance. Review the image before sharing.

These providers handle information under their own policies. Opening a news source or other external link takes you to that provider’s website.

Storage and security

Guardian stores saved API keys in encrypted form and uses HTTPS for mobile requests to its backend. The server decrypts the key when making authenticated requests to MyFundedPerps. Session credentials are stored using the device’s secure storage.

Profile, connection, preference and trading review records remain in the active database until you delete your Guardian account. Disconnecting removes the saved API key and connection settings. Notification delivery records are cleaned up after seven days. Authentication sessions expire and expired records are cleaned up during database initialization.

Deleting an app does not delete its server-side account. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and account deletion

  • Notifications: change your choices in Guardian Settings, or turn off notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Disconnect: choose Disconnect API key in Guardian Settings to remove the saved connection. You can also revoke the key in MyFundedPerps API Key Settings.
  • Delete your Guardian account: open Settings → Delete Guardian account and confirm. This removes your mobile profile, saved connection, sessions, associated trading review records and registered push devices from the active Guardian database.

Deleting your Guardian account does not close positions or delete your MyFundedPerps account. Manage those directly with MyFundedPerps. A separately created Telegram Guardian profile is also separate from your mobile profile.

For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, use the contact information on our support page. We may need to verify your account before acting on a request.

Changes and questions

We will publish changes to this policy here and update the date above. For questions about this policy or your information, contact Funded Guardian support.
