Common questions

I can’t sign in with Apple or Google.

Check your connection and try again using the same provider you used to create your Guardian profile. Apple and Google sign-ins can create separate profiles. If the problem continues, include the error message and your app version in a support request.

How do I connect my account?

Open Guardian Settings and connect your MyFundedPerps API key. Generate and manage keys in MyFundedPerps API Key Settings. Use a key for the intended live or sandbox environment. Your Apple or Google login creates your Guardian profile; the trading connection is separate.

I’m not receiving notifications.

Open Guardian Settings and check your Pulse notification choices. Then open iOS Settings → Notifications → Guardian and allow notifications. Check Focus and Scheduled Summary if alerts arrive silently or late. Open Guardian again to refresh your device registration.

My account, chart or P&L looks out of date.

Check your connection, return to the screen and confirm the selected account and market. If refreshing fails, check your account directly on MyFundedPerps. Include the market, time and any error message in a support request.

A trade or position update returned an error.

Check the order and position status on MyFundedPerps before trying again, especially after a timeout. An unclear response does not always mean the action failed. The platform determines available leverage, trading limits and order acceptance. Guardian support can help investigate the mobile experience; contact MyFundedPerps for platform account or execution issues.

How do I disconnect or revoke my API key?

Choose Disconnect API key in Guardian Settings to remove the saved connection. This does not close your positions. To revoke the key itself, use MyFundedPerps API Key Settings.

How do I delete my Guardian account?

Open Settings → Delete Guardian account and confirm. This removes your mobile Guardian profile, saved connection, sessions, associated trade review records and registered push devices. It does not close positions or delete your MyFundedPerps account. Deleting the app alone does not delete your Guardian account.

If you cannot sign in, contact support for help with an account deletion request. A separate Telegram Guardian profile is not deleted by the mobile account action.
