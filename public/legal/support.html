Support

Help with Guardian on iOS and Telegram.

Effective October 20, 2026

Get started

Join the iOS TestFlight beta or open the Telegram mini app. Connected trading requires an active MyFundedPerps account and an API key you are authorized to use.

Connection or trading trouble

Check that your API key is valid and the account is active. If a request has an uncertain result, check positions and orders directly in MyFundedPerps before retrying. Never include API keys, passwords or session tokens in screenshots or messages.

Notifications

Allow notifications in device settings and turn on the categories you want in Guardian Settings. Changes save automatically. News and alerts require a network connection and can be delayed.

Delete or disconnect

To delete your mobile account, sign in, open Settings, choose Delete Guardian account, then tap Delete to confirm. If you cannot sign in, contact the project for help. To remove just the saved key, choose Disconnect API key and confirm. You can also revoke the key directly in MyFundedPerps. Your positions and MyFundedPerps account remain with MyFundedPerps. Read the Privacy Policy for separate Telegram and website records.

Contact

DM us on X @FundedGuardian for help with Guardian or to make a privacy request. For technical problems, include your app version, device type and what happened. Never send passwords or API keys.
