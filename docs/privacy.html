Privacy Policy

How Funded Guardian handles your information.

Effective October 20, 2026

Who this policy covers

Funded Guardian is an independent companion for MyFundedPerps. This policy covers our mobile app, Telegram mini app and project websites. MyFundedPerps, Apple, Google, Telegram and hosting providers also have their own privacy policies for their services.

Information we process

  • Sign-in: Apple or Google account identifier and email address when provided; a provisioned email login may also be available. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. Provisioned passwords are verified against a salted hash.
  • Telegram: your Telegram user ID and available profile information when you open the mini app.
  • Connected accounts: an encrypted copy of the API key you connect, its last four characters, environment and selected account ID.
  • Trading: account balances, rules, positions, orders, prices, fees and trading history retrieved from MyFundedPerps; saved trade, close and protection review records and submission results.
  • Preferences and notifications: risk controls, quick-trade settings, push token, notification choices and delivery-related information.
  • App usage: app versions with analytics report a generated installation ID, platform, app version and launch timestamps. After sign-in this ID may be linked to your Guardian identity. These records support aggregate adoption and submitted-notional reporting. They are not verified App Store download counts.
  • Website signup: the email and marketing choice you submit through our beta signup service, plus signup and preference-update timestamps.
  • Service operations: requests, IP addresses and technical logs may be processed by us and our hosting providers for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

Why we use this information

We use information to authenticate you, connect your account, display account and market information, process the actions you initiate, prevent duplicate submissions, send notifications you enable, operate beta access, troubleshoot problems and understand aggregate app usage. Marketing email permission is optional and separate from beta access.

Sharing and service providers

Trading requests and your connected API key are sent to MyFundedPerps to perform requested functions. Sign-in uses Apple or Google as selected. Push delivery uses Expo and Apple services, and supported Android releases may use Google push services. Our backend and database are hosted on Railway; this static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Telegram processes Telegram interactions. News links take you to independent publishers. We may disclose information where legally required or to protect the service and its users.

We do not sell your personal information or use advertising identifiers for cross-app behavioral advertising. An external affiliate purchase is handled by the linked provider, under its own terms and privacy practices.

Storage and security

Connected API keys are encrypted in our database, and session tokens are stored using hashes on our server. Mobile session credentials use device secure storage. Access controls protect private administrative metrics. No transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve problems and meet applicable obligations. Operational logs and backups may remain until their normal replacement or expiry. This policy does not promise immediate deletion from third-party systems or backups.

Your choices and deletion

Change risk controls and notification choices in Settings. You can also disable push permission in device settings. Disconnect your API key in Guardian and revoke it directly in MyFundedPerps when you no longer want access.

Use Delete Guardian account in the mobile app Settings to remove your mobile profile, saved connection, sessions, associated review tickets and registered push devices from the active database. Analytics installation records may remain with the account link removed. A Telegram Guardian profile is separate; request its deletion through project support. Website signup records are separate from mobile account deletion.

Deleting Guardian does not close trades or delete your MyFundedPerps account. Manage positions and platform accounts directly with MyFundedPerps.

For access, correction, deletion, marketing withdrawal or a question, use our support contact. We may ask you to verify control of the affected account. Do not send API keys or passwords. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. Essential account and security processing is needed for connected features.

Children and international processing

Guardian connected trading services are intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly solicit personal data from children. Our providers may process information in countries other than yours.

Policy changes

We will publish material changes on this page and update the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a change applies.
